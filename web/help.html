Install

Fabric Loader 0.19.5+ Fabric API Java 25

The launchers below handle Java for you. Get the jar for your Minecraft version from the download page.

Prism Launcher

  1. Create an instance for your Minecraft version and pick Fabric as the loader.
  2. Edit the instance, open Mods, choose Download mods and add Fabric API.
  3. Drag the Arachne.fyi jar into the same Mods list.

Modrinth App

  1. Create a Fabric profile for your Minecraft version and add Fabric API from the Discover tab.
  2. Open the profile, choose Add content, then Add from file, and pick the Arachne.fyi jar.

Official launcher

  1. Run the installer from fabricmc.net for your Minecraft version.
  2. Put Fabric API and the Arachne.fyi jar in your .minecraft/mods folder.
  3. Pick the Fabric profile in the launcher and play.

How it works

  1. 1

    Install the mod

    It reads your chat and the loot that appears after a fight, in Arachne's Sanctuary only.

  2. 2

    Farm as normal

    The mod counts crystals, kills, drops and time spent, and shows them on screen.

  3. 3

    See it on the dashboard

    If you've been invited, pair the mod and your numbers appear on the website, valued at Bazaar prices.

Questions

Can I get notifications on my phone?+

Yes, if you're invited. Open the dashboard on your phone, use "Add to Home Screen", then turn notifications on in Settings.

Can I use the mod without an invite?+

Yes. Without pairing, the mod tracks your own drops and shows them in game. Nothing is sent anywhere. The website dashboard is the only part that needs an invite.

Why is the dashboard invite only?+

It runs on one person's PC for a group of friends. Logging in with Discord asks the admin to let you in.

How do groups work?+

A group has up to 5 members. Its owner picks how people join: invite only, public, or open to anyone who meets the requirements. Everyone in the group sees the group's totals.

Will this get me banned?+

The mod doesn't play for you, click for you, or change how your game talks to Hypixel. It only reads what's already on your screen. Hypixel's rule is that any mod not on their Allowed Modifications list is used at your own risk, and this one isn't on it. So: low risk, not zero.

What counts as a kill?+

You did at least 10,000 damage to the boss, shown in the "Your Damage" line within 5 seconds of it dying.

When does "today" start?+

Midnight UTC. In the UK that is midnight in winter and 1am in summer.

Where do the prices come from?+

The Hypixel Bazaar, checked every 5 minutes. Each drop is valued at the price when it dropped, not today's price. Items not on the Bazaar use their NPC sell price.

Why are there two "per hour" numbers?+

One is per real hour, the other per hour of one account farming. With two accounts at once, the second is about half the first.

What the mod sends

Only once you pair it with a dashboard token. Before that, nothing leaves your PC.

  • Your Minecraft name and UUID, so your accounts can be told apart.
  • When an Arachne spawned and died, and your damage line for that fight.
  • Which summon item you placed (a crystal or a calling).
  • The item ID and count of each drop you got, and Scavenger coins.
  • The mod version, your Minecraft version, and whether you're in the Sanctuary or in a fight.

It does not send your chat, other players' names, your inventory, or anything from outside the Sanctuary.

On the dashboard you can download everything stored about you from Settings. Ask the admin to delete it. The privacy notice has the details.
