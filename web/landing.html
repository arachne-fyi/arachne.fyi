Questions

Is it allowed on Hypixel?+

It only reads what your game already shows you and never plays for you. Mods outside Hypixel’s allowed list are used at your own risk, so we call it low risk, not zero.

Do I need an invite?+

The mod works for everyone and keeps local totals. The dashboard is invite only for now and opens to everyone soon.

Can my co-op see my numbers?+

Members of your groups can. You choose whether anyone else can, and you can hide yourself completely.

Which drops does it price?+

Every Arachne drop, valued at Bazaar instant sell, sell offer or NPC price. Ironman profiles always use NPC prices.
