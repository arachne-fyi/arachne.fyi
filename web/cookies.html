Cookie notice

Last updated 17 November 2026 (version 2026-11-17). We only use storage the site needs to work, so there is nothing to opt in to. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

Cookies

  • al_session: keeps you logged in. HttpOnly, secure, expires after 30 days or when you log out.
  • oauth state: a short-lived cookie that protects the Discord login step. Deleted once you are logged in.

Other browser storage

  • Settings (local storage): number style, currency, background, goal and similar choices.
  • Offline copy (service worker cache): the site and your last numbers, so it opens without a connection.
  • View as (session storage, admins only): which person an admin is looking at.

If we ever add more

If we add anything that is not strictly needed, such as analytics or ads, we will ask first, with a "Reject all" button as easy to use as "Accept".
