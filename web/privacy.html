Who is responsible

What we collect

  • From Discord when you log in: your Discord ID, display name and profile picture. We do not get your email or password, and we don't keep Discord's access token.
  • From the mod (only once you pair it): your Minecraft username and UUID, and Arachne events: crystals placed, fights, damage, drops, coins, time in the Sanctuary, mod version. Nothing outside Arachne's Sanctuary.
  • What you add: group names, tags, comments, reactions, challenge titles, settings, a Discord webhook if you add one, push subscription if you turn on phone notifications.
  • Technical: your IP address passes through our server and Cloudflare to deliver the site and to stop abuse. It is kept in short-lived server logs only.
  • Your agreement: which version of the terms you accepted and when, and that you confirmed you are 13 or over.

Why, and our legal basis

  • To run the service you asked for (contract): your account, dashboard, groups, overlay, notifications.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the service secure, preventing abuse, fixing bugs, and the anonymous totals described below.
  • Consent: phone notifications and a public profile, which you can turn off any time.
  • Legal obligation: keeping records we must keep, for example about deletion requests.

Anonymous totals

The home page shows totals across everyone: coins, drops and kills. These are sums with no names or accounts in them, and every account counts towards them. This cannot be switched off. If you delete your account, your share stays in the sums as a number only; it can no longer be traced to you.

Who can see your numbers

  • Members of groups you join can see your numbers in that group, and people on the same site can see you on the leaderboard and feed.
  • You can hide yourself from other people in Settings. You will still see your own numbers, and you still count in the anonymous totals.
  • A public profile and a stream overlay only exist if you turn them on.

Who we share it with

  • Cloudflare (network and security) handles traffic to the site.
  • Discord, for login, and only if you add a webhook, for the messages you ask us to post.
  • Your browser's push service (Google, Apple or Mozilla), if you turn on phone notifications.
  • We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising. We share it with authorities only when the law requires.

International transfers

Some of these providers process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on UK adequacy regulations or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and numbers: until you delete them, or 24 months after you were last active.
  • Server logs with IP addresses: up to 30 days. Backups: up to 30 days after something is deleted.
  • A note that a deletion happened (no name or ID): kept to show we did it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, restrict or object to how we use it, and withdraw consent. In Settings you can download everything (JSON and CSV) and delete your account yourself. You can also email contact@arachne.fyi; we reply within one month.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). In the EU you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Arachne.fyi is for people aged 13 and over. If you think a younger child has an account, email us and we will delete it.

US states

We do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising, and treat every request under US state privacy laws the same way as the rights above.

Security

Logins and mod tokens are stored only as hashes; the site uses HTTPS and strict browser security settings. If a breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the ICO without delay.
